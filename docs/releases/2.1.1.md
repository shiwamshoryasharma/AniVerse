# AniVerse 2.1.1

Explore audio filters and SUB/DUB tags on shared anime hover cards.

- 🔊 **Audio inside Explore's filter panel.** Choose **All audio**, **DUB + SUB** or **SUB (including DUB)**. The dropdown and ALL/DUB/SUB buttons share one state, survive reload and clear with Reset filters.
- 🏷️ **Clear hover tags.** Available SUB is green and DUB follows the theme accent. Explicitly unavailable audio and unverified audio have separate muted states.
- 🌐 **Confirm unviewed titles.** Catalogue cards immediately use their source counts. Other previews can search the configured source's public pages and confirm the exact mapped title, without starting an episode or requiring watch history.
- 🎬 **Honest episode totals.** AniList totals use an episode icon rather than a microphone; totals are not evidence of a dub.
- 🔒 **Preserve private sessions.** Lookups stay in the isolated source browser. Permanently private titles skip automatic persistent-session searches; incognito provides the temporary session for a private check.
- 📐 **Keep previews visible.** Hover cards measure their height and remain within the window.

Source availability is source-specific. An unreadable page, unmatched metadata ID or missing badge stays unverified; it is not presented as proof that no dub exists. Source players, navigation and normal/4K playback retain their existing paths.

Validation: TypeScript/lint, 251 unit tests, 19 integrated development UI checks, a fresh native live-source hover/filter check, signed-package integrity checks and 15 production runtime checks. The live test confirmed SUB and DUB on an unviewed ordinary title and 12 DUB catalogue cards. Physical TV/audio and installer/update installation were not tested.
