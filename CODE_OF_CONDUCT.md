# Code of Conduct

AniVerse is a community project made by and for people who love anime. We want everyone who takes part (in issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews or anywhere else this project lives) to feel welcome and safe.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone. That holds regardless of:
- age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity or nationality;
- sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, or sexual identity and orientation;
- level of experience, education or socio-economic status;
- personal appearance, race, caste, colour or religion.

## What we expect

- Be kind, patient and respectful, especially to newcomers.
- Give and receive feedback gracefully; critique code and ideas, never people.
- Assume good intent, ask before assuming, and accept responsibility for mistakes.
- Keep discussions on topic and in English where possible so everyone can follow.
- Respect people's time: search before filing, and give clear reproduction steps.

## What we don't accept

- Harassment, insults, slurs, trolling, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, including sexualised depictions of characters who are or appear to be minors.
- Sharing others' private information (doxxing) without their explicit permission.
- Spam, advertising, or repeated off-topic posts.
- **Posting links to, lists of, or requests for unlicensed streaming websites or pirated content.** AniVerse ships no sources, and this repository is not the place to share them.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

The maintainer, [@shiwamshoryasharma](https://github.com/shiwamshoryasharma), is responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct.

Depending on how serious the behaviour is and whether it repeats, responses range from:
1. a private or public warning;
2. a temporary ban from interacting with the project;
3. a permanent ban.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, report it privately to the maintainer. Use the contact options on the maintainer's GitHub profile. You can also open a minimal issue that asks for a private channel, without details. Reports are handled confidentially, and reporters are protected from retaliation.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces. It also applies in public spaces when someone officially represents the project.
